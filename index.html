ABOUT FACTUAL MANAGEMENT SYSTEMS

About Factual Management Systems

Factual Management Systems (FMS) is a compliance and audit consultancy supporting manufacturers with legal requirements, social compliance, certification support and buyer audit readiness.

FMS was started in June 2014 by Mr. DelipKumar B., Managing Consultant. Since its establishment, the company has focused on providing practical compliance support to manufacturing facilities and helping them prepare for audits, maintain required documentation and address compliance requirements.

FMS works closely with companies to understand their requirements, identify compliance gaps, prepare necessary documentation and support them through the audit and corrective action closure process.

SEDEX / SMETA
A widely used social compliance audit methodology (2-Pillar or 4-Pillar) covering labour standards, health & safety, environmental practices and business ethics. Key areas: Labour & human rights. Health & safety. Environmental practices. Business ethics. Working hours & wages.
amfori BSCI
A social compliance monitoring system used by international buyers to assess working conditions across supply chains. Key areas: Workers’ rights. Fair remuneration. Occupational health & safety. Environmental protection. Ethical business behaviour.
SA8000
An international certifiable social accountability standard covering workplace conditions and workers’ rights, aligned with ILO conventions. Key areas: Child & forced labour safeguards. Health & safety. Freedom of association. Working hours & compensation. Management systems.
WRAP
A certification program focused on lawful, humane and ethical manufacturing, primarily used in apparel and footwear supply chains. Key areas: Compliance with laws. Prohibition of forced/child labour. Health & safety. Environmental compliance. Customs compliance.
SLCP
A shared assessment framework that allows one social & labour audit to be reused across multiple buyers, reducing audit duplication. Key areas: Convergence across buyer programs. Labour rights. Health & safety. Standardised data collection.
WCA (Workplace Conditions Assessment)
An entry-level social compliance assessment used to evaluate baseline labour and safety conditions in a facility. Key areas: Labour standards. Health & safety. Wages & working hours. Management systems.
ICS Social Audit
A social audit framework used mainly by French and European retailers to evaluate supplier labour practices. Key areas: Working conditions. Health & safety. Labour rights. Compliance with local law.
RBA (Responsible Business Alliance)
A code of conduct widely used in electronics and manufacturing supply chains covering labour, health & safety, environment and ethics. Key areas: Labour. Health & safety. Environment. Ethics. Management systems.
Disney ILS Audit
Disney’s International Labor Standards audit, required for facilities manufacturing Disney-licensed products. Key areas: Labour standards. Health & safety. Environmental compliance. Facility & subcontractor disclosure.
Fair Trade Social Audit
An audit verifying fair trade principles including fair wages, safe working conditions and community benefit. Key areas: Fair wages & pricing. Working conditions. Environmental practices. Community development.
Buyer Code of Conduct (CoC) Audits
Custom audits conducted against an individual retailer’s own code of conduct and supplier requirements. Key areas: Buyer-specific labour standards. Health & safety. Environmental requirements. Documentation review.
GOTS
Global Organic Textile Standard — certifies the organic status of textiles from harvesting through environmentally and socially responsible processing. Key areas: Organic fibre content. Chemical restrictions. Environmental management. Social criteria.
OCS
Organic Content Standard — verifies the organic material content of a product through the supply chain. Key areas: Chain of custody. Organic fibre verification. Transaction certificates.
GRS
Global Recycled Standard — verifies recycled content and tracks it through the supply chain, with social and environmental criteria. Key areas: Recycled content verification. Chain of custody. Social & environmental practices. Chemical restrictions.
RCS
Recycled Claim Standard — verifies recycled input material and traces it through production. Key areas: Recycled content verification. Chain of custody tracking.
RWS
Responsible Wool Standard — certifies wool from farms that meet animal welfare and land management requirements. Key areas: Animal welfare. Land management. Chain of custody.
RAF
A responsible sourcing standard covering animal welfare and traceability requirements for animal-derived fibres. Key areas: Animal welfare. Traceability. Farm-level practices.
FSC
Forest Stewardship Council — certifies responsibly sourced wood, paper and forest-based materials. Key areas: Responsible forestry. Chain of custody. Sustainable sourcing.
PEFC
Programme for the Endorsement of Forest Certification — verifies sustainably managed forest sources for wood-based materials. Key areas: Sustainable forestry. Chain of custody.
Textile Exchange CCS
Content Claim Standard — verifies the presence and amount of a specific input material in a final product through chain-of-custody certification. Key areas: Material content verification. Chain of custody. Transaction records.
RMS
A responsible sourcing standard covering traceability and welfare requirements for a raw material in the supply chain. Key areas: Traceability. Chain of custody. Supplier verification.
ISO 9001
The international standard for quality management systems, focused on consistent product/service quality and continual improvement. Key areas: Quality management system. Process control. Customer satisfaction. Continual improvement.
ISO 14001
The international standard for environmental management systems, helping organisations manage environmental responsibilities systematically. Key areas: Environmental policy. Legal compliance. Resource use. Continual improvement.
ISO 45001
The international standard for occupational health & safety management systems. Key areas: Hazard identification. Risk assessment. Worker participation. Incident management.
ISO 50001
The international standard for energy management systems, supporting systematic improvement of energy performance. Key areas: Energy policy. Energy use monitoring. Efficiency targets. Continual improvement.
Higg FEM
Higg Facility Environmental Module — a self-assessment tool measuring a facility’s environmental performance across energy, water, waste and chemicals. Key areas: Energy & GHG emissions. Water use. Waste management. Chemical management.
ZDHC Assessment
Zero Discharge of Hazardous Chemicals — assesses wastewater and chemical management against ZDHC guidelines. Key areas: Wastewater testing. Chemical inventory. MRSL compliance.
OEKO-TEX
Certifies textiles and materials tested for harmful substances, helping ensure product safety. Key areas: Harmful substance testing. Product safety. Chemical compliance.
Better Cotton (BCI)
Verifies cotton sourced through more sustainable farming practices under the Better Cotton Standard. Key areas: Water stewardship. Soil health. Reduced chemical use. Farmer training.
C-TPAT
Customs-Trade Partnership Against Terrorism — a U.S. supply chain security program covering facility and cargo security. Key areas: Physical security. Cargo integrity. Personnel security. Procedural security.

OUR PROCESS

How We Work With You

Five steps from your first enquiry to a smoothly closed-out audit.

01

Audit Consulting & Planning

You reach out and book audit consulting support. We understand your company, factory operations, applicable standards, buyer requirements and audit scope.

02

Factory Visit & Gap Discussion

Our team visits the factory and reviews conditions with management — health & safety, legal compliance, documentation and workplace practices — flagging areas that need improvement.

03

Legal Compliance & Required Certifications

Based on the factory requirements, we coordinate and support the client in obtaining the required legal certificates, statutory documents, testing reports, approvals, and other applicable legal compliance requirements.

04

Audit Date & Documentation Preparation

We coordinate the audit schedule and prepare documentation in advance — records, policies, procedures, registers and training records — organized and ready on time.

05

Audit Support & Closure

We support you throughout the audit, coordinate with the factory team, help address observations, and support corrective action through to smooth closure.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What compliance audits does FMS support?

Factual Management Systems supports social compliance audits, buyer-specific audits, sustainability and textile certifications, and technical and management system audits, along with legal certification and environmental testing.

Do you provide SEDEX / SMETA audit consulting?

Yes. We support factories with SEDEX / SMETA (2-Pillar and 4-Pillar) audit preparation, documentation and closure support.

Do you support GOTS, GRS and other certifications?

Yes. We support GOTS, GRS, OCS, RCS, RWS and other sustainability and textile certifications, including documentation preparation and coordination with the certification body.

Do you provide factory legal compliance support?

Yes. We coordinate and support factories in obtaining statutory legal certificates, licenses and approvals required for compliant operation.

Do you support manufacturers outside Tiruppur?

Yes. While our team is based in Tiruppur, Tamil Nadu, we support manufacturers and exporters across India.

Do you support audit finding closure?

Yes. We support corrective action planning and follow-up so audit findings and observations are addressed and closed out.
